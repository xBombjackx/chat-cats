Chat Cats

A stream overlay that gives chat something to do while you're starting up, AFK or just chatting. Viewers type !cat and get a little voxel cat that lives on your stream: it eats, naps, picks friends and rivals, sneaks up on people, races, and gets into boxes. Dogs, raccoons and otters too.

Streamer buttons

Made for dead air

Starting-soon, BRB, and just-chatting screens where your face is small and the scene is the content. Chat keeps itself busy; you don't have to run it.

They have opinions

Every animal gets a secret personality and secret likes and dislikes. Friends hang out and share bowls; rivals hiss, stalk each other, and fight over food. Nobody configures it.

Chat plays

!cat to spawn and style, !pet, !sneak, !tackle, !race with !join, !name, !afk. Twitch and Kick chat, no bot account needed.

You run events

Race with Twitch Predictions, feeding time, treat cannon, box drop, vacuum, doorbell, cucumber. Buttons, Stream Deck URLs, or channel point rewards.

Subs, bits, raids

Subs get a party hat and confetti, bits rain fish, a raid drops a crate of boxes and a crew of visitor cats.

Rooms and species

Bedroom, kitchen, living room, garden, or a transparent overlay on top of your own scene. Cats, dogs, raccoons, otters, or all at once.

Runs as an OBS browser source from a small Node server on your PC (or one I host for you while it's in pilot). Persistent cats, cooldowns, no-go zones for your webcam, a companion page viewers can click on. Interested in trying it on your stream?
