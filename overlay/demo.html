Chat Cats

A stream overlay that gives chat something to do while you're starting up, AFK or just chatting. Viewers type !cat and get a little voxel cat that lives on your stream: it eats, naps, picks friends and rivals, sneaks up on people, races, and gets into boxes. Dogs, raccoons and otters too.

Click the scene and press H to show the controls and play around: spawn your own animal, type commands as a chatter, fire the streamer events, switch rooms. Fake chat keeps things moving in the meantime.

Runs as an OBS browser source from a small Node server on your PC (or one I host for you while it's in pilot). Persistent cats, cooldowns, no-go zones for your webcam, a companion page viewers can click on. Interested in trying it on your stream?
